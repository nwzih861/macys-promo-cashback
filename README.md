## Why your Macy’s promo code may not work

A Macy’s code that appears current can still be rejected. The most common causes are straightforward.

### The item is excluded

Designer brands, beauty products, jewelry, electronics, and special collections often have separate rules. Check the product page and the offer terms rather than relying on the department name.

### The order does not meet the minimum

A code such as “$20 off $100” requires the qualifying subtotal to reach $100 after exclusions. Gift cards, shipping, taxes, and excluded merchandise may not count toward the threshold.

### The item is already part of a special promotion

Macy’s identifies several types of promotional pricing, including Limited-Time Specials, Deals of the Day, DoorBusters, clearance, and other reduced-price events. Some promo codes cannot be combined with these prices.

### The code is account-specific

Email and app offers may be linked to a particular Macy’s account. A code sent to one customer may not work for another account.

### You are shopping in the wrong channel

Macy’s states that online and store promotions do not always match. A store coupon may not work on macys.com, while an online code may not be accepted at a physical store.

### Another offer is already applied

Macy’s allows only one promo code per order. If the Shopping Bag has already applied a code, you may need to remove it before testing a different one.

### The promotion has ended

Macy’s offers can change without much warning. A page indexed by a search engine may still show an old code even after the promotion has closed.
